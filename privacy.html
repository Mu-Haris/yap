privacy policy

last updated: May 14, 2026

this is the privacy policy for yap. ("the app"), an iOS application published by Kinex Apps ("we", "us", "our"). we built yap. to teach Gen Z slang gently — and we want to be just as gentle with your data.

1. information we collect

we collect the minimum data needed to make the app work:

  • account info. when you sign up with email/password or Google Sign-In, we store your email and display name via Firebase Authentication.
  • app activity. your daily streak, words collected in your jar, quiz accuracy, and notification preferences. stored locally and synced via Firebase Cloud Firestore so it follows you across devices.
  • device identifiers. when permitted, Apple's Advertising Identifier (IDFA) is shared with Google AdMob to show relevant ads. you control this through the App Tracking Transparency prompt on first launch — denying it disables tracking but the app still works.
  • diagnostics. Firebase may collect crash and performance data so we can fix issues.

2. how we use it

  • to keep you signed in and sync your jar across devices
  • to schedule the daily reminder notifications you opt into
  • to show ads (unless you've purchased the ad-free upgrade)
  • to fix bugs and improve the app

we do not sell your personal information.

3. advertising

the free version of yap. shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device identifier and limited diagnostic info to serve ads. you can opt out of personalized ads through your iOS settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising) or by declining the App Tracking Transparency prompt in the app.

you can permanently remove ads with a one-time $1.99 in-app purchase ("yap. tender").

more on AdMob's data practices: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. in-app purchases

purchases are processed by Apple. we do not see your payment details — we only receive a verified receipt confirming the purchase. you can manage or restore purchases in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions/Purchase History.

5. third-party services

  • Firebase Authentication — sign-in (Google, Apple). privacy
  • Firebase Cloud Firestore — cross-device sync of your jar and streak. privacy
  • Google AdMob — advertising. privacy
  • Google Sign-In — optional sign-in. privacy
  • Apple StoreKit — in-app purchases. privacy

6. children

yap. is not directed at children under 13. if you believe a child has provided us with personal info, contact us and we'll delete it.

7. your rights

you can request that we delete your account and any associated data at any time. just email us at the address below.

8. data retention

account and sync data is retained while your account is active. when you delete your account, we delete it within 30 days.

9. changes

if we change this policy materially, we'll update the "last updated" date above and, where appropriate, let you know in-app.
