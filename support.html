frequently asked

how do I restore my "remove ads" purchase on a new device?

open the app → tap you in the bottom bar → tap the settings icon → tap restore purchases on the "yap. tender" card. as long as you're signed in with the same Apple ID, your purchase will come back.

I bought "remove ads" but still see ads

first try restore purchases (see above). if that doesn't fix it, fully quit the app and reopen. still nothing? email us with your Apple ID order number and we'll figure it out.

how do I turn off daily reminders?

go to settings → daily reminder time inside the app and toggle reminders off. or disable notifications for yap. in iOS Settings → Notifications → yap.

how do I change my email or password?

settings → account section → tap email or change password. you'll be asked to re-enter your current password for security.

can I delete my account and data?

email muhammadharisgift1@gmail.com from the email on your account and we'll delete it within 30 days.

I forgot my password

on the login screen, tap forgot password? and follow the email link.

do you offer refunds?

refunds are handled by Apple. request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

which iOS versions are supported?

yap. supports iOS 17 and later, on iPhone and iPad.
